A one-line weather forecast for a city that does not exist, written for the
[Funniest Forecast for a City That Isn't](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-forecast-for-a-city-2a8769cf)
bounty on make.fun.

> Today in Mizzleford: rain falls upward, high 14°C, puddles overhead. Umbrellas to be worn on the feet.

## How it works

Run `node main.ts`. It creates `/output` if needed and writes the forecast —
a single line, 102 characters, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`.

Mizzleford is an invented city where the rain falls upward, so the puddles
gather overhead and the official advice is to wear your umbrella on your
feet. The forecast borrows the flat phrasing of a weather bulletin and then
breaks it.
